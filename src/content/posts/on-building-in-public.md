There's a temptation to wait until something is "ready" before sharing it. A cleaner design, a more polished demo, a better explanation. But waiting for perfection often means waiting forever.

## The case for messy work

Sharing early and often has a few advantages:

1. **Feedback loops shorten.** You find out what resonates (and what doesn't) much faster.
2. **Accountability increases.** Public commitments are harder to abandon than private ones.
3. **Others can learn from the process**, not just the result.

The best technical blogs I've read aren't showcases of finished products — they're honest accounts of how something came together, dead ends included.

## What "public" means here

Building in public doesn't mean livestreaming every keystroke. It means:

- Writing about what you're working on, even if it's unfinished
- Sharing code before it's perfect
- Documenting decisions, not just outcomes

This blog is part of that practice. Some posts will be polished; others will be rough drafts dressed up just enough to publish. That's the point.
