This is the first post on the blog. Think of this space as a public notebook — a place for long-form writing, quick notes, and the occasional experiment.

## Why a blog?

Writing forces clarity. Putting ideas into words, even rough ones, sharpens thinking in a way that just *reading* or *discussing* never quite matches.

## What to expect

Posts here will cover a mix of topics:

- **Tech notes** — things I've learned, built, or broken
- **Project write-ups** — deeper dives into side projects
- **Miscellaneous** — whatever seems worth sharing

No fixed schedule. Posts show up when they're ready.

## The stack

This site is intentionally minimal: React, Vite, and Cloudflare Pages. Blog posts are plain Markdown files that get rendered at build time. No CMS, no database, no fuss.

Thanks for reading. More to come.
